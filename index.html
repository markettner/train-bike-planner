❓ How to use Train + Bike

Instead of having to cycle loops every time, Train + Bike helps you find cycle routes that start at a train station outside the city and then cycle back home. You define your target cycling distance and the route profile, and the app finds train connections to take you to the perfect starting point.

Route Settings

  • Distance & Tolerance: Set your preferred bike ride distance and tolerance. The app matches stations whose cycle route back home sits within this range.
  • Bike Profiles: Select Trekking, Road bike, or Gravel bike to use customized routing preferences and travel time estimates.
  • Additional Routes: Toggle to see routes slightly outside your search settings. These soft matches will be along other train lines and are shown as translucent, faded lines on the map and have dashed borders in the sidebar.
  • Train Line Overlay: Toggle the background rail network overlay using the 🚆 button above the zoom controls (bottom-left).

Route Sidebar

  • Merged Lines: Multiple train lines serving the same station are combined on a single card to keep the list clean.
  • Travel Time & Frequency: Shows transit duration and how frequently trains run (e.g. Hourly, Frequent, or 2h intervals).
  • Utilization Badges: Displays live passenger occupancy expectations (Low, Medium, or Packed ⚠️).
  • Bike Carriage: A 🚲 no bike badge means at least one leg of that connection does not take bicycles — hover it to see which line. Most connections carry bikes, so only the exceptions are marked.
  • Locked Routes: Fallbacks requiring a bus transfer (where bicycles are generally restricted) are marked with black-and-yellow hazard warning stripes.

Details & GPX Export

  • Connection Timelines: Click any card to see transfer steps, platforms, connection times, and live disruption guidance (critical warnings for cancelled regional services).
  • Alternative Departures: Lists upcoming departures and highlights emptier connections with a "Recommended" badge when their occupancy is lower.
  • GPX Export: Save a cycle route using the download icon on a card, or click "Export all routes as GPX" in the settings panel to download all candidate paths together in a single multi-track GPX file.
